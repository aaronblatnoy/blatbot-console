Roles

A role's scopes run for every person who holds it. A role with a send scope is called out: Blatbot can speak as itself for that person without Aaron seeing it first.

Scopes

The tree Blatbot walks to decide what a request may use, left to right: system, then the question asked (or "always"), then the scopes granted, then the tools inside each scope. Click any box to fold or unfold what hangs off it; hover for its description. Role names next to a scope are the roles that hold it.
